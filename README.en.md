What is it for?

Chat Project is a simple chat to talk with friends and coworkers directly between computers, sending messages and images with no account and no server storing your conversations. It started as a study project about networking and JavaScript, with a look inspired by Discord.
How does it work?

You and your friend join the same room, with the same name and password. Everyone in the room shows up in your contact list, and your browser opens a direct connection (WebRTC) to theirs. Messages and images travel straight from one to the other. Public servers only help you find each other at the start. Your history is saved in your own browser.
Step by step

    Agree with your friend on a room name and a password (through any app).
    Click "+ Join a room" and type the name and the password.
    Your friend does the same, with the same name and the same password.
    When they show up in the list with a green dot, they are online. Pick the contact and start chatting.
    Use the paperclip 📎 to send images. Right-click a contact to delete it.

Good to know

    Both people need to have the page open to exchange messages.
    Contacts show up on their own, but only for people who joined the same room with the same password.
    History is stored only in this browser. Clearing the site's data erases everything.
    On very restrictive networks, or with extensions that turn WebRTC off, the connection may fail. If that happens, a notice appears at the top of the page.
    If the contact does not show up: check that the room name and password are exactly the same on both sides (case matters), that you both have the page open, and that the top shows "Ready to chat". Open the site from its GitHub Pages address, not from the file on your computer.

Privacy

Messages go straight between browsers, encrypted, and do not pass through a server. The public introduction servers can see that a room exists and the network addresses of whoever connects. Use a password only you two know: anyone who knows the room name and password can join it.
