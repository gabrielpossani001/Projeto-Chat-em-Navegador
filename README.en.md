What is it for?

LAN Chat is a simple chat to talk with friends and coworkers directly between computers, sending messages and images with no account and no server storing your conversations. It started as a study project about networking and JavaScript, with a look inspired by Discord.
How does it work?

Each person gets an ID. When you connect to someone's ID, your browser opens a direct connection (WebRTC) to theirs. Messages and images travel straight from one to the other. A public server only helps the two of you find each other at the start. Your history is saved in your own browser.
Step by step

    Copy your ID at the top of the page and send it to your friend (through any app).
    Ask for their ID and click "+ Connect by ID".
    Paste the ID, give them a nickname (optional) and confirm.
    When the contact's dot turns green, they are online. Pick the contact and start chatting.
    Use the paperclip 📎 to send images. Right-click a contact to delete it.

Good to know

    Both people need to have the page open to exchange messages.
    There is no automatic contact discovery. Exchanging IDs takes its place.
    History is stored only in this browser. Clearing the site's data erases everything.
    On very restrictive networks the connection may fail.

Privacy

Messages do not go through a server, but the introduction server can see the IDs and network addresses of whoever connects. Do not share your ID with people you don't know: anyone with your ID can ask to chat (you can always decline).
